WHAT IS JUAFIT?

Turn “what next?” into a plan.

A career guidance companion for learners choosing their next education step. Start with your interests and academic profile, then explore your options.

Find courses that fit you

Get a ranked course shortlist with suitability percentages and reasons behind each match. Exploring school pathways? JuaFit helps with that too.

See where you could study

Compare university programmes and printed subject requirements from the KUCCPS 2025/2026 catalogue, with links to the source.

Talk it through with JuaGuide

Ask questions about courses and careers. Choose to share academic results for a more personal conversation about your next step.

Guidance to support your decision. Suitability scores are provisional when assessments are incomplete; they do not guarantee admission.
